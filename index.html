Our Story

Rooted in Tradition. Made for Today.

At Aaranya, we celebrate India's rich culinary heritage through authentic flavours, traditional recipes, and a welcoming modern cafe experience. Every cup of chai and every plate of food carries a story — of home kitchens, of grandmothers' recipes, and of the warmth that defines Indian hospitality.

We bring together the soul of an Indian street chai stall and the calm of a contemporary cafe — earthy textures, warm light, and the comforting aroma of freshly ground spices.
